## Background

Rapid identification of MRSA carriers is critical for infection control. Conventional culture methods require 24–48 hours, delaying decolonisation and contact precautions. LAMP technology offers a culture-independent approach with sub-hour turnaround.

## Results

| Parameter | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Sensitivity | 97.4% (95% CI 91.8–99.3) |
| Specificity | 99.1% (95% CI 97.2–99.8) |
| PPV | 97.4% |
| NPV | 99.1% |
| Turnaround | 45 min |

## Conclusion

This LAMP assay is a reliable, rapid alternative for MRSA screening in clinical practice, potentially reducing transmission by enabling same-day contact precautions.
