## Abstract

*Candida auris* is an emerging multidrug-resistant fungal pathogen associated with high mortality in immunocompromised patients. This multicenter study describes the first Brazilian series of *C. auris* infections across three tertiary-care ICUs.

## Methods

Prospective surveillance identified *C. auris* isolates using MALDI-TOF MS and species-specific PCR. Antifungal susceptibility was determined by EUCAST broth microdilution. Whole-genome sequencing was performed for clonal analysis.

## Results

- 41 patients identified over 24-month period
- Crude 30-day mortality: 61%
- Echinocandin resistance (FKS mutations) in 23% of isolates
- Two distinct genomic clusters consistent with nosocomial transmission

## Conclusions

The emergence of *C. auris* in Brazilian healthcare settings demands urgent implementation of antifungal stewardship and enhanced infection control protocols.
