## Abstract

Carbapenem-resistant *Klebsiella pneumoniae* (CRKP) poses a critical threat to patient safety in healthcare settings. Using whole-genome sequencing (WGS) and core-genome MLST, we identified a dominant ST258 clone responsible for 78% of CRKP bloodstream infections over a 24-month period, harboring *bla*KPC-2 on a conjugative plasmid.

## Key Findings

- ST258 clone dominated CRKP isolates (78% of bloodstream infections)
- *bla*KPC-2 carried on conjugative IncF plasmid
- ICU-to-ward transmission via shared healthcare workers
- Real-time WGS surveillance enabled targeted infection control interventions

## Significance

This study demonstrates the feasibility and impact of implementing genomic surveillance in a resource-limited setting, providing actionable data for infection control teams.
