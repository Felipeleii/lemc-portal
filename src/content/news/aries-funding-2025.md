## ARIES Funding Renewal

We are thrilled to announce that the **ARIES (Antimicrobial Resistance Identification & Epidemiological Surveillance)** project has received a three-year funding renewal from FAPESP (São Paulo Research Foundation), grant #2025/00001-0.

### What's Next

The renewed funding will support:

- **Expansion to 5 new hospital sites** across São Paulo state and the Federal District
- **Enhanced WGS capacity** with on-site Oxford Nanopore sequencing for real-time outbreak detection
- **Development of an open-access dashboard** for AMR trend visualisation
- **Training programme** for infection control professionals in genomic surveillance methods

### Team Growth

The project will recruit two additional postdoctoral researchers and four PhD students beginning in March 2025.

### Impact

ARIES data will feed into the Brazilian National AMR Action Plan (Plano Nacional de Combate à Resistência Antimicrobiana) and be shared with the WHO Global Antimicrobial Resistance and Use Surveillance System (GLASS).
