## Welcome to the New Cohort

The LEMC/ALERTA research group is delighted to welcome four new graduate researchers to our team, joining the Postgraduate Program in Infectious Diseases at UNIFESP.

### New Members

**MSc Students**
- Researcher in bioinformatics and phylogenomics (advisor: Dr. Lei)
- Researcher in clinical epidemiology of fungal infections (advisor: Dr. Streling)

**PhD Students**
- Doctoral candidate in rapid diagnostics and CRISPR-based detection methods
- Doctoral candidate in antimicrobial stewardship outcomes research

### Research Directions

The new cohort will contribute to active ARIES sub-projects and open collaborative opportunities within the Brazilian AMR research network.

We look forward to the scientific contributions of this talented group!
