## LEMC/ALERTA at ECCMID 2025

The European Congress of Clinical Microbiology and Infectious Diseases (ECCMID) 2025 brought together over 14,000 researchers and clinicians in Vienna, Austria. The LEMC/ALERTA team had a strong presence with multiple presentations.

### Oral Communications

1. **"Genomic surveillance of KPC-producing *Klebsiella pneumoniae* in Brazilian ICUs: 2-year ARIES data"** – presented by Dr. Ana Paula Streling
2. **"*Candida auris* clonal outbreak in a neonatal ICU: molecular characterisation and infection control response"** – presented by Dr. Felipe Lei
3. **"LAMP-based point-of-care MRSA screening: a prospective multisite validation"** – presented by Dr. Pedro Neves

### Poster Presentations

- **"Colistin heteroresistance in *Acinetobacter baumannii*: implications for clinical management"**
- **"Environmental persistence of *C. auris* on healthcare surfaces: a longitudinal sampling study"**

### Highlights

The ARIES project data attracted significant attention from European surveillance networks, with two potential collaborative agreements initiated with institutions in Portugal and Spain.
